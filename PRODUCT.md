## Platform

## Users

Owners and managers of small and medium businesses (1 to 50 people) in the Eje Cafetero: Armenia, Quindío, Pereira and Manizales. Restaurants, tourism and lodging, retail, services and independent professionals. They run their operation on Excel, WhatsApp and manual steps, and they look for a website, custom software or process automation when that stops scaling. Confirmed by the owner on 2026-10-15.

## Product Purpose

Arkanis Solutions is a technology company that designs websites, builds custom software ("software a la medida") and automates business processes. arkanis.site exists to win local searches ("desarrollo web Armenia", "software a la medida", "automatización de procesos con IA") and turn visitors into requests for a technology diagnosis (`/contacto`). Success is a qualified diagnosis request from a local business.

## Positioning

A local team in Armenia, Quindío that builds software fitted to how each business actually operates, instead of forcing generic tools, and ships real products it runs in production (Komio, Directorio Terrario).
