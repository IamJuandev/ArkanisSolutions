Scope: arkanis.site homepage (`/`), Persuade mode. Related: Komio product page.

Audience: owners of 1–50 person businesses in Armenia and the Eje Cafetero running on Excel and WhatsApp. Job: understand that Arkanis builds AI-powered custom software affordably, see real proof (Komio, Directorio Terrario), and request a free diagnosis. Constraints: official brand package (owner-pinned, not rolled), no invented clients/metrics/prices, Komio AI shown as "Próximamente", Spanish voseo copy.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The homepage is Arkanis's commercial-proposal cover made interactive; it refuses the dark-tech agency template of glowing cards and the generic split hero with a badge row.

OWN-WORLD: Azul Noche #061A2E ground, Niebla #F2F7FB light bands, Azul Arkanis #0B5FE0 for links, Verde Señal #17C46E only for actions and "on" states, the official 135° green→blue gradient as 3px strips, 1px hairline frames, square corners, Space Grotesk headings, IBM Plex Sans body, IBM Plex Mono for data labels and the title block.

STORY: A visitor sees what Arkanis does and where, picks a task they hate doing by hand, watches the arc between the blue node (their business today) and the green node (their system) light up with an illustrative fix, then sees Komio and Terrario running, how AI vs. simple automation is chosen, the process, and asks for the free diagnosis by form or WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Hairline-framed cover; left 60%: H1 with the main keywords, a short lead, the "¿Qué te gustaría dejar de hacer a mano?" chooser, the two-node arc and the green "Pedí tu diagnóstico gratis" button plus WhatsApp link; right 40%: the 3D "A" render masked into the ground; bottom: a four-cell title block (Komio, Terrario, Armenia, 1–50 people).

FORM: Owner-pinned brand world (the commercial proposal cover), chosen after the rolled directions were rejected; original roll seed 6d20cd03 not used.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Signature interaction: the node arc drawing in (stroke-dashoffset, exponential ease-out) when a task is chosen; reduced motion shows it instantly.
